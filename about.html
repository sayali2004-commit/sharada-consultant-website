About Us

Two decades of building trust, quality, and architectural excellence

Our Journey

From Vision to Legacy

Sharada Consultant and Developers was founded by Shekhar Parit with a simple yet powerful vision: to create construction that stands the test of time while exceeding client expectations.

Starting as a small firm in Baramati, Maharashtra, we have grown into one of the most trusted names in construction and development. Our journey has been marked by unwavering commitment to quality, innovation, and client satisfaction.

Over the years, we have successfully delivered over 150 projects across residential, commercial, and institutional sectors. Each project reflects our dedication to craftsmanship and our ability to adapt to evolving architectural trends.

Meet The Founder

Shekhar Parit

Shekhar Parit is the driving force behind Sharada Consultant and Developers. With over two decades of experience in construction and development, he has built a reputation for excellence, integrity, and innovation.

His deep understanding of construction technology, combined with his passion for creating spaces that inspire, has made Sharada Consultant a preferred choice for discerning clients.

Shekhar Parit believes in building not just structures, but relationships. His hands-on approach and commitment to client satisfaction have been instrumental in the company's growth and success.
